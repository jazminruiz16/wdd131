About Us

At EcoTierra Bolivia, we are dedicated to growing and distributing high-quality organic vegetables. Our mission is to promote healthy eating and sustainable agriculture that respects the environment and supports local communities.

Founded in 2021, we have grown with the commitment of small farmers who believe in ecological production. We work hand in hand with families from the rural areas of Cochabamba to offer fresh, pesticide-free products.
